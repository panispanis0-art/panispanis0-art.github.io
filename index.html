LudusTown

LudusTown is a private assistant that one person runs on their own computer, to help with their own work. It isn't a public service, and nobody can sign up for it.

Its owner can connect their own Google account, so it can read their email and calendar, read their Drive, and save its finished reports to a folder of its own in their Drive. On Google's sign-in screen it appears under the name "Jarvis".
