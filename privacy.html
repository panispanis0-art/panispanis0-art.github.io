Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

LudusTown (shown as "Jarvis" on Google's sign-in screen) is a private assistant that one person runs on their own computer. It isn't offered to the public. The only Google account it uses is its owner's.

What it can reach in a Google account

  • Gmail, read only: who unread emails are from, their subject, date and a short preview.
  • Google Calendar, read only: events in the next 7 days.
  • Google Drive, read only: the names, dates and links of recent files.
  • Google Drive, saving: it adds its own finished reports to its own folder. It can't change or delete files it didn't create.

It can't send email, or change or delete calendar events.

The owner's second private assistant uses the same Google app and asks for more: it can read and draft email, manage calendar events, and work with Sheets and Drive files. It follows the rest of this policy too, except that its data can also go to the AI services and tools its owner sets it up with.

How that data is used

Only to show the owner their own information and to help them with their own work. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.

Where it goes

  • The sign-in stays on the owner's computer, locked in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Claude, by Anthropic: when the owner asks for a brief, a copy of the data above is sent to Claude to write it. Claude is what powers the assistant.
  • Nowhere else: Google data isn't shared with anyone else or sent to any server run by LudusTown. There isn't one.

LudusTown's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long it's kept

Email, calendar and file details are read when needed and aren't stored by LudusTown. A brief is shown on screen and not saved. Reports saved to Drive stay there until the owner deletes them.

Removing access

Access can be ended at any time from the LudusTown Connections page, or in the Google Account under third-party access.
